Karigari

Every piece passes through the same hands, thread by thread.

Behind every DRZL bag is a karigar seated at a wooden frame, working pearls and zari into velvet the way it's been done for generations. Nothing is printed — every motif is stitched.

We work from a small studio in Udaipur, keeping runs deliberately limited so each bag gets the attention it needs before it reaches you.
